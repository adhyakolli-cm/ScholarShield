Scholarship scam red flags

Guaranteed selection
Claims that make guaranteed award promises deserve careful verification.
Upfront fees
Unexpected application, processing or deposit requests are important warning signals.
Unsolicited awards
Be cautious about awards you did not apply for.
Sensitive data requests
Verify why banking, identity or other sensitive information is being requested.
Impersonation
Look closely at domains, spelling, branding and contact details.
